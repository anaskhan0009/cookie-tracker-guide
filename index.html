🍪 Cookies & trackers, in plain language

A cookie is a small note a website asks your browser to keep. Some notes help you (staying logged in). Others follow you around the web building an advertising profile.

🏠 First-party cookiesSet by the site you are visiting. Used for logins, baskets, preferences. Mostly necessary and mostly harmless.
🕵️ Third-party cookiesSet by embedded outsiders — ad networks, analytics, social buttons — on sites they don't own. This is the main tracking mechanism: the same network sees you on thousands of sites.
⏱ Session vs persistentSession cookies vanish when you close the browser. Persistent ones survive for months or years — that's how 'recently viewed' follows you.
👆 FingerprintingWhen cookies are blocked, trackers combine your screen size, fonts, timezone and hardware into a near-unique ID. Blocking cookies helps; it does not make you invisible.
